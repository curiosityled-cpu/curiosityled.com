# Implementation hours log

One row per launch, kept with the tenant record. The guardrail is five hours.
There is no target below it and no tolerance above it without a written reason.

| Tenant | Package | Kickoff | Live | Ph1 | Ph2 | Ph3 | Ph4 | Ph5 | Ph6 | Total | Over/under 5 h | Cause if over |
| ------ | ------- | ------- | ---- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ----- | -------------- | ------------- |

## Rules

- Hours to one decimal. A phase with no time against it did not happen.
- The cause column is completed whenever the total exceeds five hours, in the
  implementer's words, not a category.
- The median across launches is the number used in the commercial review, never
  the best launch.
- The first launch sets the baseline; it is not treated as an outlier to be
  explained away.
