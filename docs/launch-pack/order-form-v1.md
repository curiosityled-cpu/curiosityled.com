# Order form v1 — scope and acceptance

The signed scope and the acceptance criteria are the same sentences. Nothing in
this document contradicts `docs/delivery-runbook.md`.

## 1. What is being delivered

- The Curiosity Led platform, on the package named below, for the manager
  population agreed at kickoff.
- Managers named as managers are counted as managers; everyone else using the
  platform is a participant, including frontline staff who submit check-ins
  through Teams or a private link. Admins who manage nobody are not counted.
- Implementation, delivered in the six phases of the runbook, within five hours.

## 2. Acceptance

The launch is complete when items 1–4 hold. Item 5 is the first month-two
commitment, not a condition of go-live.

1. The agreed manager population has been invited, and at least one manager has
   completed a check-in in the live tenant.
2. The agreed check-in cadence is switched on and has delivered at least one
   automated prompt to a manager.
3. The agreed report layout exists and shows real tenant data.
4. The manager training session has been delivered and the manager quick-start
   and visibility guide have been sent.
5. The 30-day review is booked at launch and held within five working days of
   day 30.

## 3. What is explicitly not included

- Any launch assurance stated in business days.
- Any published AI or Atreus usage allowance.
- Any connector beyond Microsoft Teams, Outlook calendar, Google Calendar and
  HubSpot.
- Content from third-party learning partners, which is priced separately.

## 4. Change control

A deviation from this scope is agreed in writing before the launch date, never
discovered at the 30-day review. A package change takes effect from the date it
is signed and never removes a capability already in use.
