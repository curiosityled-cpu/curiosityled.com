# Month-one report template

The layout every launch configures, so an implementer other than the author can
set it up the same way. The worked example below is **fictional**.

## Layout

One page, four blocks, in this order:

1. **Participation** — managers who checked in this week, out of managers
   invited. One number and a seven-day bar.
2. **Attention this week** — the people or goals that need a conversation, each
   with the reason it surfaced (no check-in for five days, goal stalled, 1:1 not
   held).
3. **Goal progress** — by team, completion percentage and how many goals are
   behind.
4. **One read** — three sentences from Atreus on what the week looks like, with
   the supporting numbers behind it.

## Worked example (fictional)

> **Participation** — 9 of 12 managers checked in this week (75%).
>
> **Attention this week** — 3 items: two managers have not checked in since
> Monday; one customer-success goal has been at 40% for three weeks.
>
> **Goal progress** — Customer Success 62% (2 behind), Operations 48% (3 behind),
> Finance 80% (0 behind).
>
> **One read** — Participation is steady but concentrated: the same three
> managers account for every missed day. The customer-success goal has not moved
> since the last review, which usually means the owner is blocked rather than
> behind.

## Rules

- Fictional data only in the example, labelled as fictional wherever it is shown.
- No individual check-in text appears in this report; it reports participation
  and goal state, not answers.
- The same layout is used in the 30-day review, so month one and month two are
  comparable.
