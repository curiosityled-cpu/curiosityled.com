# Manager quick-start

One page to hand a manager on day one.

## What a check-in is for

A check-in takes about a minute. It asks how the day went, what got in the way,
and what you want to protect tomorrow. It is not a status report and nobody is
scored on it.

## How it works

- You get one prompt a day, in the app or by email, at the time your
  organisation agreed.
- Answer what is useful to you. You can skip a question; the check-in still
  counts as done.
- Miss a day and you can fill it in up to a week later, if your organisation has
  left that window open.

## What you get back

- Your own rhythm: what your week looks like across days, and where it is
  drifting.
- Atreus, the manager companion, reads your recent check-ins and offers one
  thing to try, not a list. You can ask it for more.
- Your team view shows who on your team has not checked in, and the goals that
  have stalled. It shows patterns, not scores of people.

## What your manager and HR can see

- Your manager sees your team's participation and goal progress — aggregates.
- Individual check-in answers are visible according to the visibility setting
  your organisation chose at kickoff. Ask your admin which one is in force; the
  visibility and approval guide has the exact wording.
- Nobody sees a check-in as a performance rating. It is not used in a review.

## Two things worth doing in week one

1. Book your 1:1s in the app, so the meeting prep has something to read.
2. Put one goal per person on the team, so progress has a shape.
