# Visibility and approval guide

Who sees what, and how a change is approved. Agreed at kickoff and written down,
not implied.

## The three visibility settings

| Setting | Your manager sees | HR sees | Anyone else |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Standard** | Their team's participation and goal progress, and individual check-in answers for their direct reports | Aggregates across the organisation | Nothing |
| **Confidential** *(default)* | Their team's participation and goal progress — aggregates only | Aggregates across the organisation | Nothing |
| **Highly confidential** | Participation counts only, no goal detail | Participation counts only | Nothing |

Individual check-in answers are never shown to someone outside the person's
reporting line unless the organisation has explicitly chosen **Standard**.

## Who can change the setting

- Only an HR admin or a platform administrator of your organisation can change
  the visibility setting.
- A change requires a second approver from your side. One person cannot widen
  visibility alone.
- A change applies from the day it is made; it does not retroactively open
  earlier check-ins.

## What is never visible, at any setting

- Check-in answers to anyone outside the reporting line.
- Individual results from a 360 review, before the review cycle is finalised.
- Any other customer's data: workspaces are separate, and a person belongs to
  exactly one organisation.

## Asking for a change

Send the request to your named owner. They record the decision, who approved it,
and the date. If the request is to widen visibility, the second approver is named
in the same record before the change is applied.
