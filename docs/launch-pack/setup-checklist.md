# Setup checklist (customer side)

What we need from you, and what we do with it. Phases 0–2 of the delivery runbook,
written for the person sending us information rather than for the implementer.

## 1. Before kickoff — send us

- [ ] The signed order form, with the package named (`essentials`, `growth` or
      `organization`).
- [ ] Your named owner: the person who will be in the kickoff and can approve
      configuration decisions.
- [ ] The outcome you want in 90 days, in your words. One paragraph is enough.

## 2. Who is in — send us

- [ ] **Managers**: everyone with someone reporting to them. Name, email, job
      title, department.
- [ ] **Participants**: everyone else who will use the platform. Same fields.
- [ ] **Frontline staff**, only if they submit check-ins without a platform
      account: name, email, team, manager, and whether they should receive their
      prompt in Teams or by private link.
- [ ] Who manages whom, if it is not obvious from the list.

## 3. What we do with it

- Import the people into your own workspace. No other customer can see them.
- Confirm the manager and participant counts against your package **before**
      sending invitations, and tell you if the list runs over.
- Send the invitations, and follow up on anyone who has not signed in during
      week one.
- Set up your three check-in templates, your goals framework and your month-one
      report layout.

## What we never do

- We never move a person into another customer's workspace.
- We never count an admin who manages nobody as a manager.
- We never switch on automated prompts before you have agreed the cadence and
  the local send time.
